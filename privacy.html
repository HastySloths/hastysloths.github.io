Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30/05/2026

This policy describes HastySloths — a private educational content creation and publishing project operated by Richard W., based in Pessac, France. HastySloths publishes original educational science videos to its owned accounts on TikTok (@hasty_sloths), Instagram (@hastysloths), and YouTube (@hastysloths).

Scope

The App is operated by and for a single owner. It does not provide a service to third-party users, does not register or authenticate any external user, and does not collect personal data from members of the public.

Data we process

  • OAuth credentials for the operator's own social media channels (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram). These authorize the App to post to those channels and are stored locally on the operator's machine.
  • Open-access research metadata (titles, authors, abstracts, DOIs) fetched from Europe PMC, used solely as source material for video scripts.
  • Aggregate engagement statistics (views, likes, comments, age-group cohorts) returned by platform analytics APIs for the operator's own posts. No individual user data is collected.

How data is used

Data is used only to (a) generate and publish original educational content to the operator's owned channels and (b) measure performance of that content to improve future posts. Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

The App interacts with the official APIs of TikTok, YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta), Europe PMC, and Anthropic (for AI-assisted script generation). Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Retention

OAuth tokens are stored only as long as the operator authorizes the App. Aggregate engagement data is retained locally for performance analysis and is not transmitted to any external party.

Your rights (GDPR)

As the App does not process data from third parties, there is no third-party personal data subject to access or deletion requests. The operator's own account data can be revoked at any time by removing the App's authorization in the respective platform's settings.
